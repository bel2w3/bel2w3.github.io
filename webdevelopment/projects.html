FEATURED WORK

Seasons (2026)

OFFICIAL SCRIPT

This capstone film is a personal exploration of my life as a Christian. This is a story shaped by divorce, a complex relationship between father-daughter, and anxiety with the our main character Elizabeth. Through a linear narrative structure, this film reflects on both my testimony, and a source of conviction and struggle. Visually driven, this project uses controlled cinematography, motivated lighting, and expressive editing to mirror internal conflict and moments of grace. Scripted fragments and observational imagery work together to convey emotional truth rather than resolution, presenting faith as a lived experience in life. Knowing we are imperfect, questioning, unique, and deeply human.

Timed Poison (2025)

OFFICIAL SCRIPT

This project was done for my Cinematography II course, my junior year of college and focuses on applying core visual storytelling principles within a controlled production setting. The film emphasizes script development, motivated lighting, and intentional cinematography all while engaging in stylistic elements related to Romanian New Wave. This project explores how minimalism and realism can shape the emotional tone of a film. This piece for me was a practice in my own career as a producer, and script supervisor. As a whole, this film was a technical exercise with and a stylistic study, allowing me to refine visual discipline while working within the limitations and philosophies of a specific cinematic movement.

Circles (2024)

OFFICIAL SCRIPT

This project was done in my junior year of college, and served as a comprehensive introduction to the process of filmmaking. It marked my first experience of writing an original script and translating it from paper to the screen through active directing and collaboration with actors. This project involved a storyboard and a detailed shot list to guide the visual storytelling and production planning. In post-production, the film was edited using Adobe Premiere Pro, allowing me to explore rhythm, pacing, narrative structure, and color grading. Overall, this piece represents my first step in my development as a filmmaker by bringing storytelling, directing, and technical execution into a creative work.
